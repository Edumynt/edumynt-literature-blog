## Quick Answer

Aristotle’s Poetics is an ancient Greek work that explains tragedy, plot, imitation, and catharsis.

## Simple Explanation

Aristotle studies how stories work. For him, tragedy imitates serious action and creates pity and fear to bring catharsis.
