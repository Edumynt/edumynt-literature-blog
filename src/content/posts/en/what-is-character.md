## Quick Answer

A character is a person, animal, or figure in a literary work who takes part in the action.

## Simple Explanation

Characters are the “people” of literature. We understand them by actions, speech, thoughts, and relationships.

## Easy Examples

Flat characters have one or two traits; round characters are complex and develop.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Know protagonist, antagonist, flat, round, static, dynamic, direct and indirect characterization.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not say flat means useless; flat characters can serve an important function.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
