## Quick Answer

Good literature notes are short, organized, revision-friendly summaries of periods, authors, works, terms, and exam points.

## Simple Explanation

Notes should help future-you revise fast. They are not a copy of the textbook; they are a clean map.

## Easy Examples

Make separate pages for movements, authors, works, terms, quotes, and previous-year questions.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Use tables, timelines, one-page author cards, definitions, examples, and mistake lists.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not make notes so long that you never revise them.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
