## Quick Answer

The Romantic Age in English literature is usually dated 1798–1832 and emphasizes imagination, emotion, nature, and individual experience.

## Simple Explanation

It begins symbolically with Lyrical Ballads in 1798. Romantic writers reacted against rigid neoclassical rules.
