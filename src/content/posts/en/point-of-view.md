## Quick Answer

Point of view is the angle from which a story is told.

## Simple Explanation

It is like the camera of the story. Who is seeing? Who is speaking? How much do they know?

## Easy Examples

First person uses “I”. Third person uses “he/she/they”; it may be limited or omniscient.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Exams ask types: first person, third person limited, third person omniscient, second person.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not confuse author with narrator; the narrator is the voice inside the text.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
