## Quick Answer

William Shakespeare was an English poet, playwright, and actor of the Elizabethan and Jacobean periods, often called the Bard of Avon.

Shakespeare matters because he shaped English drama with unforgettable characters, powerful language, and deep knowledge of human nature.
