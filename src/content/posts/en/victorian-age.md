## Quick Answer

The Victorian Age is the period of Queen Victoria’s reign, 1837–1901, marked by industry, empire, social reform, and the novel.

## Simple Explanation

Victorian literature often asks: What is progress costing society? It looks at class, morality, science, faith, and urban life.
