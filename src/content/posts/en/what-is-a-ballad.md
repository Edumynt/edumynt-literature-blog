## Quick Answer

A ballad is a narrative poem or song that tells a simple, dramatic story.

## Simple Explanation

A ballad is like a story people can sing. It often has repetition, dialogue, and a fast-moving plot.

## Easy Examples

Traditional folk ballads and Coleridge’s The Rime of the Ancient Mariner show ballad qualities.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Remember: narrative, simple language, refrain, dialogue, supernatural or tragic events.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not confuse ballad with any love song; literary ballads are story-poems.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
