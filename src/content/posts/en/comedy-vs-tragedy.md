## Quick Answer

Comedy generally ends happily and exposes human weaknesses; tragedy ends seriously, often with suffering or death.

## Simple Explanation

Comedy says, “Humans are foolish but life can continue.” Tragedy says, “A serious fall can teach us something deep.”
