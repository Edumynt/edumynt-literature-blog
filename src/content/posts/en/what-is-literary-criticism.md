## Quick Answer

Literary criticism is the study, interpretation, evaluation, and analysis of literature.

## Simple Explanation

Criticism is not just finding faults. It asks how a text works, what it means, and why it matters.

## Easy Examples

Aristotle’s Poetics, feminist criticism, Marxist criticism, formalism, reader-response, and postcolonial criticism are examples.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Mention interpretation, evaluation, theory, close reading, context, and approaches.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not use criticism as only negative opinion; in literature it means serious analysis.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
