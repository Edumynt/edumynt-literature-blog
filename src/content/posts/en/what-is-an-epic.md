## Quick Answer

An epic is a long narrative poem about heroic deeds, grand events, gods, nations, or civilizations.

## Simple Explanation

An epic is a huge story-poem. Its hero often represents a culture, not just one person.

## Easy Examples

Homer’s Iliad and Odyssey, Virgil’s Aeneid, Milton’s Paradise Lost, and Indian epics like the Mahabharata and Ramayana.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Features: vast setting, heroic central figure, elevated style, supernatural elements, and serious theme.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not call every long poem an epic; grandeur and heroic scale are essential.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
