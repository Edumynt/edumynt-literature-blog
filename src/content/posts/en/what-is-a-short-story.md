## Quick Answer

A short story is a brief prose narrative focused on one main incident, mood, or conflict.

## Simple Explanation

It is like one bright window into life. There are fewer characters and a tighter plot than a novel.

## Easy Examples

O. Henry’s stories, Chekhov’s stories, and many R. K. Narayan stories show compact storytelling.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Mention unity of effect, limited characters, single conflict, climax, and concise ending.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not expect a short story to explain a whole lifetime like a novel.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
