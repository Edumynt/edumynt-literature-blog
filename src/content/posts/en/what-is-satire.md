## Quick Answer

Satire uses humor, irony, exaggeration, or ridicule to criticize human foolishness or social problems.

## Simple Explanation

Satire laughs, but it laughs for a reason: to expose what is wrong.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not treat satire as only comedy; its purpose is criticism.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
