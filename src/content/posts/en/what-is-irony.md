## Quick Answer

Irony is a contrast between appearance and reality, words and meaning, or expectation and result.

## Simple Explanation

Irony is when the text quietly says, “Look again—things are not what they seem.”

## Easy Examples

Verbal irony says the opposite; situational irony gives an unexpected result; dramatic irony lets the audience know more than a character.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not confuse irony with coincidence; irony needs meaningful contrast.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
