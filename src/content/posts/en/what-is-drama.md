## Quick Answer

Drama is literature written to be performed by actors before an audience.

## Simple Explanation

A drama is not just read; it is acted. Dialogue, conflict, stage action, and performance make the story alive.

## Easy Examples

Shakespeare’s Hamlet is tragedy; A Midsummer Night’s Dream is comedy; modern one-act plays are shorter dramas.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Remember key elements: plot, character, dialogue, conflict, setting, stage directions, and theme.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not call every story drama. Drama has performance as its central feature.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
