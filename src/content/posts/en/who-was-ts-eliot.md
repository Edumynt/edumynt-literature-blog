## Quick Answer

T. S. Eliot was a major modernist poet, critic, and dramatist known for fragmented form, allusion, and spiritual crisis.

## Simple Explanation

Eliot’s poetry often feels like a broken city of voices. That brokenness is part of its modern meaning.
