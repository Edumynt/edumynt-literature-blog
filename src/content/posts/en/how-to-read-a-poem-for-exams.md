## Quick Answer

To read a poem for exams, identify the speaker, situation, theme, tone, images, sound devices, and structure.

Read the poem like solving a friendly puzzle: first understand the surface, then notice repeated words, feelings, and patterns.

## Easy Examples

For a nature poem, ask what nature does in the poem: background, teacher, symbol, or emotional mirror?

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Use steps: title, speaker, paraphrase, theme, devices, form, context, conclusion.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not start with memorized biography before understanding the poem itself.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
