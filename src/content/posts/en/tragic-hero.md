## Quick Answer

A tragic hero is a noble or important character who falls because of a tragic flaw, mistake, or fate.

## Simple Explanation

A tragic hero is not simply a bad person. The hero has greatness, but one weakness opens the door to disaster.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not call any sad character a tragic hero; the fall must be central and meaningful.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
