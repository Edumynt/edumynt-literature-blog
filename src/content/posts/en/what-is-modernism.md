## Quick Answer

Modernism is a twentieth-century movement that experiments with form to express fragmentation, uncertainty, and modern life.

## Simple Explanation

Modernist writers felt the old forms were not enough for a broken modern world. They changed narrative, language, and structure.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not confuse modernism with anything “modern” in date; it is a specific literary movement.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
