## Quick Answer

Poetry is literary expression using rhythm, sound, imagery, and concentrated language.

## Simple Explanation

Poetry says more with fewer words. It uses music of language and pictures in the mind.

## Easy Examples

Lyric, sonnet, ode, elegy, ballad, epic, blank verse, and free verse are poetic forms.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Learn elements: speaker, imagery, rhythm, rhyme, meter, tone, theme, and figures of speech.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not say all poetry must rhyme; free verse may not rhyme.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
