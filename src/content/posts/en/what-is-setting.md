## Quick Answer

Setting is the time, place, and social environment in which a literary work happens.

## Simple Explanation

Setting is the story’s address plus atmosphere. It tells us where, when, and under what conditions events occur.

Victorian London, rural Malgudi, a battlefield, a classroom, or a future world can be settings.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not treat setting as decoration only; it can shape conflict and character.
