## Quick Answer

Theme is the central idea, message, or insight about life in a literary work.

## Simple Explanation

Theme is what the story is really about under the surface. The plot is the road; theme is the meaning of the journey.

## Easy Examples

Love, ambition, nature, corruption, freedom, death, identity, and social injustice can be themes.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

To identify theme, ask: what idea keeps returning, and what does the text suggest about it?

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not write theme as one word only. “Ambition destroys judgment” is stronger than “ambition”.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
