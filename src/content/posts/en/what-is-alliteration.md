## Quick Answer

Alliteration is the repetition of the same initial consonant sound in nearby words.

## Simple Explanation

It makes language musical and memorable, like a small drumbeat at the start of words.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not count repeated vowels as alliteration in the usual strict sense; that is often assonance.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
