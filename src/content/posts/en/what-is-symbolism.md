## Quick Answer

Symbolism is the use of an object, person, place, or action to suggest a deeper meaning.

## Simple Explanation

A symbol is one thing that points to another. A dove can suggest peace; darkness can suggest fear or ignorance.

## Easy Examples

The road may symbolize life’s journey; a chain may symbolize slavery; spring may symbolize renewal.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Exams ask definition plus examples. Explain both literal and deeper meaning.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not force every object to be a symbol; context must support the meaning.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
