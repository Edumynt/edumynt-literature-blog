## Quick Answer

Lyric poetry is a short poem expressing personal feelings, thoughts, or mood.

## Simple Explanation

A lyric is like someone singing their heart in words. It usually focuses on emotion rather than a full story.

Many Romantic poems by Wordsworth and Keats are lyrical in feeling.

Exams ask its features: subjectivity, musical quality, brevity, emotion, and single mood.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not confuse lyric with lyrics of only modern songs; lyric is an old literary form.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
