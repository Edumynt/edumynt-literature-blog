## Quick Answer

A novel is a long prose narrative about characters, events, society, and human experience.

## Simple Explanation

Think of a novel as a big story-house. It has many rooms: plot, characters, setting, conflict, theme, and point of view.

## Easy Examples

Pride and Prejudice, The Guide, Malgudi Days stories are prose fiction; The Guide is a novel by R. K. Narayan.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Exams ask features: length, prose form, realism, character development, social background, and narrative structure.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not define a novel only by page count; its prose narrative and developed world matter more.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
