## Quick Answer

An ode is a serious lyric poem that praises or reflects deeply on a person, object, idea, or event.

## Simple Explanation

An ode looks at something carefully and respectfully, almost like saying, “You matter; let me think about you.”

## Easy Examples

Keats wrote famous odes such as Ode to a Nightingale and Ode on a Grecian Urn.

A good exam example should be short, accurate, and directly connected to the definition. Do not write a famous name unless you can explain why it fits.

## Exam Angle

Mention elevated tone, address, reflection, and lyrical quality.

For competitive exams, prepare a 3-line answer:

1. definition,
2. two features,
3. one example.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not treat ode as only praise; many odes include thought, tension, or sadness too.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
