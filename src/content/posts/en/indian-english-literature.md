## Quick Answer

Indian English Literature is literature written in English by Indian writers or about Indian experience.

## Simple Explanation

It carries Indian realities through the English language: colonial history, nation, family, caste, gender, city, village, and identity.

## Easy Examples

R. K. Narayan, Mulk Raj Anand, Raja Rao, Kamala Das, Nissim Ezekiel, and Arundhati Roy are important names.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not call it British literature just because it is in English. Its cultural centre is Indian.
