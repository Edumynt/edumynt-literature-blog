## Quick Answer

British literature is commonly studied through periods such as Old English, Middle English, Renaissance, Neoclassical, Romantic, Victorian, Modern, and Postmodern.

## Simple Explanation

Periods are like shelves in a library. They help us place writers and works in historical context.
