## Quick Answer

Postmodernism questions fixed truth, stable meaning, grand narratives, and traditional boundaries of art.

## Simple Explanation

Postmodern writing often plays games with form. It may mix genres, quote other texts, and remind us that it is artificial.

## Common Mistakes and Confusions

Do not write that postmodernism means “anything goes”; it has recognizable techniques and ideas.

Also avoid mixing the term with nearby terms. When two terms look similar, compare their function: what does each term do inside a poem, play, or story?
